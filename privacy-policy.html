What this website collects

The website has no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools. Fonts and images are served from the site itself, so your browser contacts no third party to display it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like every web host, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We do not receive or use this data. See GitHub's privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you write or call us, we use your name, contact details and message to answer you and to arrange work together. Our legal basis is your request to talk with us and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)). We keep this information as long as the conversation or the working relationship lasts, and afterwards only as long as the law or a legitimate business need requires. We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, a copy of your data, or object to our processing. You may withdraw any consent at any time. Write to us at the address above and we will reply within one month.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law, such as California, you have comparable rights to know, delete and correct your data. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our team works from the United States and Europe. Where data travels from the EEA to the United States, we rely on safeguards recognised under GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.
